Solving a Route
  • A drone routes itself across generated terrain, with the solved path traced in cyan.
    •  
  • Water and rock are impassable, so the route bends around lakes rather than crossing them.
    •  
  • The status bar reports the start and end cells, the resulting path length, and whether the drone is moving or stopped.
Larger Worlds
  • The same solver runs on much bigger maps — coordinates past 390 by 314, against roughly 130 on the smaller ones.
    •  
  • Longer routes cost more to search, and the readout makes that visible: 352 steps against 103.
    •  
  • Terrain and destinations are regenerated per run, so no two searches face the same map twice.
