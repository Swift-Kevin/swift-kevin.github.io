Team Role: Main Programmer

Development Time: Game jam, Spring 2024

About
  • Play as a spaceship fighting against infected swarms of geometric enemies. The goal being to eliminate the enemy swarms before time runs out.
  • This was a Game Jam project completed during the Spring of 2024 featuring swarms/flocking AI systems.
  • Building the flocking system under jam time pressure meant favouring a simple, readable implementation over a fully general one.
